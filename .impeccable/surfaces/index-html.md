Scope: landing page `index.html`. Mode: Persuade. Audience: Recife/RMR families leaving rent, arriving from Instagram on phones. Action: open WhatsApp for a free simulation. Proof: process clarity, educational Reels, Victor's face and presence; no invented stats or testimonials. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a conversation with the specialist, not a bank brochure. It refuses the institutional bank-site look (logo wall, stock family photo, generic icon grid) and the guru-sales page.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy #0B1F3A night field for the hero and the closing block; off-white #F7F8FA reading ground in between; institutional blue #005CA9 as the working ink for icons, links and step numbers; orange #F39200 exclusively on CTAs with navy text. Sora bold headlines with tight tracking, Inter body. Large radii (24–32px), soft offset shadows, glass reserved for the cards floating over Victor's photo and the scrolled header.

STORY: Visitor recognises their own doubts typed as chat messages, sees Victor answer, understands the 4-step path, watches him teach (Reels), meets him, and taps WhatsApp or fills the 4-field form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy field. Mobile: headline (Sora, ~2.4rem) top, subheadline, full-width orange CTA "Fazer minha simulação grátis", secondary link; Victor's cut-out portrait below on a blue radial glow with three glass chips. Desktop: copy left 6/12, portrait right with chips orbiting.

FORM: Pinned by the user's brief. seed: none (pinned) — "Paleta: azul-marinho profundo (#0B1F3A) como base, azul institucional (#005CA9) e um laranja de destaque (#F39200) só nos CTAs… títulos em Sora… glassmorphism sutil nos cards… Foto profissional do Victor, recortada, com fundo em gradiente azul e cards flutuantes".
