# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + Tailwind CSS v3 (compiled to a single CSS file, no runtime CDN) + vanilla JS. Chosen from the user's brief ("HTML + Tailwind ou React + Tailwind"); static wins for load speed in the Instagram in-app browser and for easy text edits. Hosted on GitHub Pages from `main`.

## Users

Families and young adults in Recife and the Região Metropolitana (RMR) who want to leave rent and buy their first home, usually financed by Caixa. They arrive mostly from Instagram (@victorramoscaixa) on a phone. They have doubts about FGTS, down payment (entrada), ITBI, Minha Casa Minha Vida and credit approval, and they fear bureaucracy and being deceived.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Victor Ramos, correspondente bancário Caixa and real-estate credit specialist, partner of Master Casa Imobiliária. Success = a visitor opens WhatsApp to ask for a free simulation / credit analysis.

## Positioning

A local specialist who follows the client personally from simulation to keys, explains financing in plain language (his videos: "Fala, pessoal… fica ligado!"), and pairs bank-correspondent work with real-estate assessoria through Master Casa.

## Operating Context

Visitors come from Reels and the bio link; the decision happens in a WhatsApp conversation. Real process: conversa e simulação → análise de crédito → documentação e aprovação → assinatura e chaves.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before persuasion: every claim verifiable, every rule caveated.
2. One action: WhatsApp, reachable from anywhere on the page.
3. Explain like Victor talks: plain words, one idea per sentence.
4. Local and personal over institutional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
